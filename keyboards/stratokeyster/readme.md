# stratokeyster

### pick guard wiring
#### 5 way pickup selector
- com -> row 3
- 1 or 6 -> col 8
- 2 or 7 -> col 7
- 3 or 8 -> col 6

#### EC11 rotary encoder 15mm half shaft
- push button -> row 3 and col 9
- encoder -> encA, gnd, encB

#### RS26 3 pole 4 throw 15mm half shaft
- com -> row 3
- 1 -> col 13
- 2 -> col 12
- 3 -> col 11
- 4 -> col 10
